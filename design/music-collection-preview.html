Six pressings

Each colour lasts two months and changes on the 1st. There is no gradual drift during the month. Tap one to preview it.

The record symbol

One disc for the header, the browser tab and the installed icon. It is drawn to sit inside a circle, so Firefox's round crop does not clip it. The installed icon cannot change colour by month, so it keeps one fixed pressing.

Three type pairings

Headings and big numbers use the display face. Everything you read at length uses the body face, and both stay easy to read. Tap one to apply it to the whole page.
